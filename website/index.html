macOS security notice


Because Phillip is not Apple-notarized, macOS may show a warning when you first open it. If that happens, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, find the message about Phillip being blocked, and click Open Anyway. Then confirm Open.


Updates: When an update occurs a notice will be displayed here. When displayed, click the blue “Download Phillip for Mac” button for the fresh new version.
